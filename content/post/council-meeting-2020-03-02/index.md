### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)**:
- **Lori Saginaw** (*Administrative Leave of Chief Cox (DC-5)*): Lori Saginaw, resident, spoke about the administrative leave of Police Chief Michael Cox. She spoke about racial inequities in Ann Arbor.
- **Peter Houk** (*Council Administrative Committee Recommendation Regarding Interim City Administrator (DC-4)*): Peter Houk, resident, spoke about the appointment of Tom Crawford as Acting City Administrator. He urged Council to give him the support he needs to serve as administrator. Mr. Houk spoke about the wasteful financial costs to terminate the contract with Mr. Lazarus.
- **Mozhgan Savabiesafahani** (*Don't Postpone Gelman Cleanup Forever (DC-1)*): Mozhgan Savabiesafahani, candidate for 4th Ward City Council, spoke about the clean-up of the Gelman 1,4 dioxane plume. She urged Council to request that voters approve a millage for clean-up.
- **Blaine Coleman** (*Don't Postpone Gelman Cleanup Forever)*): Blaine Coleman, resident, urged Council to ask the public to support a millage for the Gelman dioxane plume clean-up.
- **Elizabeth Collins** (*Gelman Site Resolution (DC-1)*): Elizabeth Collins, Ward Five resident, talked about the City's recent test results that showed 1,4 dioxane in the City's drinking water. She stated the City knows the polluter is Gelman and they need to make him clean the pollution.
- **Geoffrey Henny** (*Center of the City Taskforce Report (AC-4)*): Not present.
- **Abu Ibrahim** (*ICPOC (INT-2)*): Abu Ibrahim, resident, asked about the placement of the Chief of Police on administrative leave. He asked for answers about the leave. He talked about racial inequities in the City.
- **Henry Herskovitz** (*CC - Communications from Council (Header on Agenda)*): Henry Herskovitz, resident, spoke about Council Communications, made on January 21, 2020. He asked why the City Council has not reached out to discuss issues his group has with Beth Israel congregation.
- **Catherine Wilkerson** (*Pledge of Allegiance (Header on Agenda)*): Dr. Catherine Wilkerson urged Council to stand in opposition to the Pledge of Allegiance, and to support the end of the production and stockpiling of nuclear weapons.
- **Erik Massey** (*Decriminalize Nature Ann Arbor DNA2*): Erik Massey spoke in support of plant-based psychedelics. He talked about the use of these plants for treatment of depression. He urged Council to support the decriminalization of these plants.

### Public Hearings
- **[`PH-1`](https://a2gov.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=4336745&GUID=9ECC51B2-8617-471E-BD66-911A3649AA89&G=505481FE-B2ED-4B34-9A92-FDB26536D1E1&Options=&Search=): Resolution to Approve The Standard at Ann Arbor Site Plan and Development Agreement at 405 South Main Street (CPC Recommendation: Approval - 9 Yeas and 0 Nays)** — The following persons were present to speak: Ethel Potts, resident, asked questions about access to the alley. Ralph McKee, Ward Five, expressed concerns about the traffic impacts from this project. The architect, representing the developer, spoke about the project and was available to answer any questions of Council. Adam Oxner, Ward 4, spoke in support of the project and the mixed-use aspects of retail and residential. There being no further comment, the Mayor declared the hearing closed.
- **[`PH-2`](https://a2gov.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=4324288&GUID=1219DD42-CF39-48E4-B9A3-C6114F116467&G=505481FE-B2ED-4B34-9A92-FDB26536D1E1&Options=&Search=): Resolution to Approve the Lohmann Annexation, 1.52 Acres, 2511 Newport Road (CPC Recommendation: Approval - 9 Yeas and 0 Nays)** — There being no one present for comment, the Mayor declared the hearing closed.

**General Public Comment**:
- Gerard Campain, Ward Three, spoke about the racism in the community and the issues surrounding the administrative leave of the police chief.
- Rev. Troy Patterson spoke about the issues in the Police Department and the racial issues in the City.
- Shirley Beckley, resident, spoke about racism in Ann Arbor and the issues with the police chief.
- A resident of Ann Arbor spoke about the recent events surrounding the termination of the City Administrator and the placement of the police chief on administrative leave. He stated the public still needs answers and Council needs to be more transparent.
- Zachary Storrey, resident, spoke about public transportation in New York City. He also spoke about the police chief being placed on administrative leave.
- A former Ward One Council Member spoke about the administrative leave of the City's police chief and the damage it has done to him and to the community.
