### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SWq1XirWDlM&t=867s)):
- **[Steven Hall on behalf of Spencer Restaurant](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SWq1XirWDlM&t=982s)** (*Resolution to Approve Downtown Street Closures for Restaurant and Retail Use During the Time of Mandated Physical Distancing (DC-2)*): Steven Hall, 113 E. Liberty, owner of Spencer Restaurant, spoke in opposition to DC-2.
- **[Evan Redmond](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SWq1XirWDlM&t=1182s)** (*Resolution to Approve the Collective Bargaining Agreement between the City of Ann Arbor and Ann Arbor Police Officers Association (CA-14)*): Evan Redmond, candidate for 3rd Ward City Council, spoke in opposition to the proposed collective bargaining agreement with the Ann Arbor Police Officers Association and the lack of independent oversight included.
- **[Landon Slanger](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SWq1XirWDlM&t=1375s)** (*Collective Bargaining Agreement between the City of Ann Arbor and Ann Arbor Police Officers Association (CA-14)*): Landon Slanger, 554 Elizabeth Street, #E1, was not present for comment.
- **Chingchi Chen** (*Resolution Directing the City Administrator to Prepare Resolutions to Approve Ballot Language for a Street, Bridge, and Sidewalk Millage (CA-8)*): Chingchi Chen, 2725 Nixon Road, spoke in support of CA-8. He urged that sidewalk projects be funded by the City as they represent a public good.
- **Marin Klumpner** (*Resolution Directing the City Administrator to Prepare Resolutions to Approve Ballot Language for a Street, Bridge, and Sidewalk Millage (CA-8)*): Marin Klumpner, 2765 Nixon Rd., spoke in support of CA-8.
- **Julie Lin** (*Sidewalk Millage (CA-8)*): Julie Lin, 2703 Nixon, spoke in support of CA-8. She asked that the Barton Hills special assessment be postponed until after the November vote.
- **Sya-Ufu Ma** (*Resolution Directing the City Administrator to Prepare Resolutions to Approve Ballot Language for a Street, Bridge, and Sidewalk Millage (CA-8)*): Sya-Ufu Ma, 36601 Mule Train Rd., Carefree, AZ, spoke in support of CA-8 and urged the City Council to change the way sidewalk gaps are funded.
- **Jane Vogel** (*Solar Faithful Initiative*): Jane Vogel, 1223 Sky Park, spoke of the Solar Faithful Initiative and how the City needs to build on the legacy of the program to help reduce emissions.
- **Ralph McKee** (*Divisiveness*): Ralph McKee, 1116 Red Oak Road, spoke on leadership and divisiveness.
