### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)**:
- **Trische Duckworth** (*Michigan Theater Charitable Gaming License (CA-1)*): Trische Duckworth, of Van Buren Township, spoke in favor of CA-1, stating approval of the charitable gaming license will allow the Michigan Theater to make revenue during the shutdown. She also spoke about Councilmember Hayner and his home renovation project. She urged him to resign from City Council.
- **Marilyn Wedenoja** (*Mental Health and Public Safety Millage*): Marilyn Wedenoja, Ward 2, urged City Council to use the Mental Health and Public Safety Millage for its intended purpose, as voted on by City voters.
- **Barb Higman** (*Mental Health and Public Safety Millage*): Barb Higman, resident, urged Council to use the Mental Health and Public Safety Millage rebate funds, as they were approved by voters.
- **Mozhgan Savabieasfahani** (*Resolution to Clarify and Modify R-20-425 (DC-3)*): Mozhgan Savabieasfahani, Ward 4, thanked City Council for removing item DC-3 from this evening's agenda.
- **Blaine Coleman** (*Resolution to Clarify and Modify R-20-425 (DC-3)*): Blaine Coleman, resident, urged Council to proceed with the Gelman clean-up and to request Super fund status of the plume.
- **John Floyd** (*Apology*): John Floyd, resident, apologized for his previous disparaging remarks to former Council Member Zachary Ackerman.
