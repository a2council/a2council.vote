### Public Comment

### Public Hearings

There were no public hearings on the agenda.

**General Public Comment** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hgrwiyQTUR8&t=7377s)):
- Zachary Storey, resident, apologized to Councilmember Hayner for his comments directed towards him at a previous meeting. He thanked Council for extending the City Administrator search process.
- Henry MacConnel, resident, applauded the Council for passing the mask ordinance. He discussed the need for additional enforcement to help prevent the City from becoming a COVID hotspot.
- A resident spoke in favor of hiring Tom Crawford, but also spoke in favor of allowing additional public commentary on the matter.
- Luis Vasquez, resident, expressed concern about the special meeting not having public commentary at the beginning of the meeting. He expressed disappointment in the entire City Administrator hiring process, starting with the firing of former City Administrator Howard Lazarus.
- Peter Houk, a resident, discussed the hiring process and how the City Administrator serves as a public facing individual, and thanked the Council for slowing the process to allow for additional commentary from City residents.
- Michelle Hughes, a resident, thanked Council for providing additional time in the City Administrator search process, and encouraged Council to take a proactive approach moving forward to engage the community.
- Anna Foster, a Ward Five resident, thanked Council for their vote to extend the amount of time the public has to participate in the City Administrator search process.
- Eric Sturgis, a Ward One resident, thanked Amy Cell for her work on the recruitment process of a new City Administrator. He asked that the City work to engage all sectors of the community and that Council listen to the input of residents.
- Joe Spaulding, California resident, discussed the need for equity in the City Administrator search process. He also discussed the University of Michigan students who have been photographed partying and not practicing social distancing.
