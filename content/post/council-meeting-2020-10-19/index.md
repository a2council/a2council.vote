### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PLq5oHiN-_U&t=2024s)):
- **[Elizabeth Collins](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PLq5oHiN-_U&t=2102s)** (*Resolution to Approve Settlement Documents in Attorney General v. Gelman Sciences (DC-8)*): Elizabeth Collins, 3404 Porter Road, spoke in opposition to the proposed consent judgment. She urged Council to consider Super Fund status for the plume.
- **James D' Amour** (*Resolution to Approve Settlement Documents in Attorney General v. Gelman Sciences (DC-8)*): James D'Amour, resident, urged Council to join Scio Township and Ann Arbor Township in requesting Super Fund status for the Gelman Plume clean-up.
- **Tom Stuhlberg** (*Resolution to Approve Settlement Documents in Attorney General v. Gelman Sciences (DC-8)*): Tom Stuhlberg, resident, spoke in opposition to the proposed Gelman consent judgment, urging Council to consider Super Fund status.
- **Shanah Milkie** (*Resolution to Approve Settlement Documents in Attorney General v. Gelman Sciences (DC-8)*): Shanah Milkie, resident, spoke about the proposed Gelman consent judgment.
- **Zachary Storey** (*Indigenous People's Day*): Zachary Storey, resident, spoke about Indigenous People's Day and thanked the City for its recognition of the day.
