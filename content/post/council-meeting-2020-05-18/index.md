### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pCg1z_swAu4&t=1755s)):
- **[Ilene Tyler](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pCg1z_swAu4&t=1841s)** (*A2Zero Carbon Neutrality Governance Plan (INT-1)*): Ilene Tyler, 126 N. Division, spoke in support of the A2Zero Carbon Neutrality Governance Plan.
- **Kathleen Murphey** (*A2Zero Carbon Neutrality Governance Plan (INT-1)*): Kathleen Murphey, 315 Linda Vista, spoke in support of the A2Zero Carbon Neutrality Governance Plan.
- **Elijah Kapcher-Kay** (*A2Zero Carbon Neutrality Governance Plan (INT-1)*): Elijah Kapcher-Kay, a Ward 5 resident and Pioneer High School freshman, spoke in support of the A2Zero Carbon Neutrality Governance Plan.
- **Laura Rubin** (*A2Zero Carbon Neutrality Governance Plan (INT-1)*): Laura Rubin, 625 Fountain St, on behalf of the Ann Arbor Climate Partnership, spoke in support of the A2Zero Carbon Neutrality Governance Plan.
- **Bentley Johnson** (*A2Zero Carbon Neutrality Governance Plan (INT-1)*): Bentley Johnson, 448 Manor Dr., on behalf of the Michigan League of Conservation Voters, spoke in support of the A2Zero Carbon Neutrality Governance Plan.
- **Clark McCall** (*A2Zero Carbon Neutrality Governance Plan (INT-1)*): Clark McCall, 3355 Yellowstone Dr., spoke in support of the A2Zero Carbon Neutrality Governance Plan.
- **Jean Luvervich** (*Resolution Regarding FY21 Budget and Financial Recovery Plan (DC-8)*): Jean Luvervich, 912 Pomona, spoke in favor of backyard chickens in neighborhoods. She also urged City Council to vote against DC-8 regarding the FY21 Budget Recovery Plan.
- **Glenn Nelson** (*Resolution to Adopt Ann Arbor City Budget (DS-2)*): Glenn Nelson, 1323 S. Forest Ave., urged Council to use the County Public Mental Health and Public Safety millage funds on mental health and public safety expenses.
- **Ron Suarez** (*Resolution in Support of the Medicare for All Act of 2019 (DC-7)*): Ron Suarez, 506 N. State St., spoke in support of DC-7 and a plan to expand Medicare for all.
- **Barbara Higman** (*Resolution in Support of the Medicare for All Act of 2019 (DC-7)*): Barbara Higman, 2835 Parkwood Ave., urged Council to use the County Public Mental Health and Public Safety millage funds on mental health priorities.

**General Public Comment** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pCg1z_swAu4&t=27592s)):
- Erica Briggs, candidate for 5th Ward City Council, voiced her concerns that the Living A2Zero Ann Arbor Carbon Neutrality Plan was postponed and that funding for pedestrian safety was reduced.
- Zachary Storey, 1315 Kensington Dr., thanked Council for approving additional funding for the most vulnerable residents at the May 4th meeting. He also discussed the impact of a recent social media post regarding affordable housing and chickens and spoke of the personal impact it had on him.
- Jamie Magiera, 1410 Henry St., thanked City Council for all of their hard work. He also discussed the impact embodied carbon has on climate change.
- Ed Vielmetti, 1210 Brooklyn Ave, expressed hope and concerns over the adopted budget. He also discussed the impact that social distancing was having on political polarization.
- Luis Vazquez, 1442 Pontiac Trail, thanked Jean Luvervich for discussing the recent social media post regarding chickens and affordable housing.
