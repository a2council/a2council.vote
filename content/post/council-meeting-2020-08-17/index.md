### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JKKrh0L-qpw&t=3110s)):
- **Peter Houk** (*Resolution to Endorse HB 4738 and the Creation of a Statewide Crosswalk Law(DC-1)*): Peter Houk, 2025 Yeoman Ct., spoke in favor of keeping the existing Crosswalk Ordinance and urged Council not to endorse HB 4738.
- **Mozhgan Savabieasfahani** (*Don’t turn Ann Arbor City Council into an army for Israel*): Mozhgan Savabiesfahani, resident, urged City Council not to be turned into an army for Israel. She also urged the Council to not allow campus to re-open this Fall.
- **Blaine Coleman** (*Don’t turn Ann Arbor City Council into an army for Israel*): Blaine Coleman, resident, urged City Council not to be turned into an army for Israel.
- **Donna Waters** (*Housing*): Donna Waters, of behalf of Survivor Speaks, discussed concerns regarding the electronic housing wait list and ensuring that an electronic format doesn't preclude individuals who aren't technologically knowledgeable from gaining access.
- **John McLaughlin** (*Stop the Hate*): John McLaughlin urged members of the public to not use public commentary to make racist or anti-Semitic statements.

**General Public Comment** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JKKrh0L-qpw&t=12086s)):
- Frances Mills, a resident, spoke about the capital expense concerns associated with Lurie Terrace, but expressed excitement with the City's acquisition of the property.
- An Attorney from Dickinson Wright, on behalf of the Lurie Terrace Board, expressed appreciation of the City's effort to purchase the property.
- Henry McConnell, a resident, spoke about the need for City to create a downtown masking requirement.
