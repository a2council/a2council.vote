### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jLaydAzelpo&t=194s)):
- **Tim Hull** (*Resolution Directing the Planning Commission to Create a Transit Supported Development District (DC-2)*): Tim Hull, 2475 Lancashire Dr., Apt 2B, urged Council to pass DC-2 and to support transit-oriented planning.
- **Kai Petainen** (*Resolution Recognizing Eppie Potts (DC-5)*): Kai Petainen, 2222 Fuller Ct., Apt 8, reflected on the contributions that Eppie Potts had on the City and urged Council to create an Eppie Potts citizens participation award, or acknowledge her years of dedicated service to the City in some other manner.
- **Alan Haber** (*Virtual Earth Day Week & Community Commons*): Alan Haber, 531 3rd St., urged Council to support the upcoming virtual Earth Day.
- **Mozhgan Savabieasfahani** (*Fight for $15.00 Minimum Wage- Vote for the Doctor*): Mozhgan Savabieasfahani, PO Box 7038, candidate for the 4th Ward City Council, discussed the need for a $15.00 minimum wage for all workers in the City of Ann Arbor. She also discussed additional action that needs to be taken by the City in response to the COVID-19 pandemic.
- **Blaine Coleman** (*PhD Savabieasfahani City Council Campaign- Vote for the Doctor.com*): Blaine Coleman, PO Box 7038, discussed the need for a field hospital to be set up in the City in response to the coronavirus pandemic. He also discussed how the City should be taking additional efforts to get action on the Gelman Plume clean-up.

### Public Hearings ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jLaydAzelpo&t=9165s))
- **[`PH-1`](https://a2gov.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=4336737&GUID=4C9AAA21-C441-4119-8D58-9B8CCE759A39&G=505481FE-B2ED-4B34-9A92-FDB26536D1E1&Options=&Search=): An Ordinance to Amend Chapter 55 (Ann Arbor Unified Development Code), Rezoning of 0.6 Acre from C2B (Business Service District) to C3 (Fringe Commercial District), MSGCU Rezoning, 2151 West Stadium Boulevard (CPC Recommendation: Approval - 6 Yeas and 0 Nays) (Ordinance No. ORD-20-04)** ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jLaydAzelpo&t=9284s)) — Ed Vielmetti, resident, spoke in favor of the proposed rezoning. There being no further comment, the Mayor declared the hearing closed.

**General Public Comment** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jLaydAzelpo&t=18275s)): Jaime Magiera, Ward 4, thanked Council for the opportunity for the public to participate in the electronic meeting.
