### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W7MYnU_P1lI&t=4059s)):
- **Tom Stulberg** (*MMG Plymouth Road Mixed Use Rezoning, 3611-3621 Plymouth Road (C-1)*): Tom Stulberg, 1202 Traver St., urged City Council to vote no on the Mixed Use Rezoning, 3611-3621 Plymouth Rd.
- **Ralph McKee** (*MMG Plymouth Road Mixed Use Rezoning, 3611-3621 Plymouth Road (C-1)*): Ralph McKee, 1116 Red Oak Rd., thanked Dr. Jackson for her powerful presentation to Council. He also discussed the need for additional affordable housing in the community.
- **[Eric Sturgis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W7MYnU_P1lI&t=4467s)** (*Resolution to Request that ICPOC Lead an In-depth, Multi-Stakeholder, and Data-driven Review of Public Safety Services and Provide City Council with Reports and Recommendations (DC-16)*): Eric Sturgis, 2009 Pontiac Trail, thanked Dr. Jackson for her speech. He urged Council to vote against DC-16.
- **[Michelle Hughes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W7MYnU_P1lI&t=4485s)** (*Resolution to Request that ICPOC Lead an In-depth, Multi-Stakeholder, and Data-driven Review of Public Safety Services and Provide City Council with Reports and Recommendations (DC-16)*): Michelle Hughes, 214 Charles St., urged City Council to de-fund the police and reinvest the money into affordable housing, mental health, and substance abuse counseling.
- **[Glen Nelson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W7MYnU_P1lI&t=4823s)** (*Resolution for Override of Mayor’s Partial Veto of R-20-177*): Glen Nelson, 1323 S. Forest Ave., urged Council to override the Mayor's partial veto of R-20-177.
- **Syaufu Ma** (*Resolution to Amend the Installments and Amounts of the Special Assessment Roll for Districts 55, 56, 57, and 60 (CA-7)*): Syaufu Ma, 36601 N. Mule Train, Carefree AZ, spoke in objection to the Nixon Rd. special assessment.
- **Joe Lin** (*Motions to Waive Council Rules (2/3 Vote) and Reconsider the May 4, 2020 Approval of Resolution 4 - Special Assessment Roll for Nixon and Traver Special Assessment District No. 56 (DC-7)*): Joe Lin, 2703 Nixon Rd., urged Council to reconsider the special assessments on Nixon, Traver, and Barton Dr., and to wait to see the result of the November vote before deciding how to fund sidewalk gaps.
- **Marin Klumpner** (*Motions to Waive Council Rules (2/3 Vote) and Reconsider the May 4, 2020 Approval of Resolution 4 - Special Assessment Roll for Nixon and Traver Special Assessment District No. 56 (DC-7)*): Marin Klumpner, 2765 Nixon Rd., urged City Council to delay the special assessment on Nixon, Traver, and Barton Dr., until the millage vote in November.
- **Keith Hampton** (*Motions to Waive Council Rules (2/3 Vote) and Reconsider the May 4, 2020 Approval of Resolution 4 - Special Assessment Roll for Nixon and Traver Special Assessment District No. 56 (DC-7) & Resolution to Amend the Installments and Amounts of the Special Assessment Roll for Districts 55, 56, 57, and 60 (CA-7)*): Keith Hampton, 415 Barton Dr., discussed the impact of COVID-19 on the ability to communicate with Council on the special assessments and asked that Council wait to levy the special assessments until after the November election.
- **William Hampton, Representing NAACP** (*Darker Side of Humanity*): William Hampton, on behalf of the local NAACP, discussed the death of George Floyd and the lack of accountability within police departments. He urged the Council to divert a portion of the police budget to social programs to address the root causes of community issues.
