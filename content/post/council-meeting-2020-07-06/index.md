### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMj4aLWGHrk&t=3511s)):
- **Glen Ziegler** (*Proposed Application Materials, Review and Recommend Development Entitlements to the City Council for 415 W. Washington to Support the Development of Affordable Housing in the City (DS-1)*): Glen Ziegler, 514 Krause, spoke in opposition to the pre-entitlement of the affordable housing at 415 W. Washington.
- **David Silkworth** (*Proposed Application Materials, Review and Recommend Development Entitlements to the City Council for 415 W. Washington to Support the Development of Affordable Housing in the City (DS-1)*): David Silkworth, candidate for the 5th Ward City Council, spoke in opposition to DS-1.
- **Joe Lambert** (*Proposed Application Materials, Review and Recommend Development Entitlements to the City Council for 415 W. Washington to Support the Development of Affordable Housing in the City (DS-1)*): Joe Lambert, 503 W. Washington, asked that DS-1 be tabled to allow for other viable proposals to be presented to the City.
- **Mozhgan Savabieasfahani** (*Resolution Supporting the Environmental Protection Agency’s Active Involvement with the Gelman Site and Encouraging its Listing of the same as a “Superfund” Site (DC-1)*): Mozhgan Savabieasfahani, candidate for the 4th Ward City Council, urged the City to start the clean-up of the Gelman Plume.
- **Blaine Coleman** (*Resolution Supporting the Environmental Protection Agency’s Active Involvement with the Gelman Site and Encouraging its Listing of the same as a “Superfund” Site (DC-1)*): Blaine Coleman, PO Box 7038, spoke in support of Mozhgan Savabieasfahani's candidacy for Ward 4 Councilmember, stating she is the environmental choice.
- **Peter Houk** (*Resolution to Endorse HB 4738 and the Creation of a Statewide Crosswalk Law (DC-11)*): Peter Houk, 2025 Yeoman Ct., spoke in opposition to DC-11, stating it undoes the progress that the City crosswalk ordinance provides.
- **Shannon Hautamaki** (*Healthy Streets (DC-3 & 4)*): Shannon Hautamaki, 1110 Brooklyn, thanked the City Parks Department for its efforts to ensure a sense of normalcy during the pandemic. She urged the passage of DC-3 and DC-4 to help allow more space to in neighborhoods for social distancing and exercise.
- **Ruth Spalding** (*Independent Community Police Oversight Commission (ICPOC) (INT-1) & Analysis of the Financial Feasibility of Developing Affordable Housing on Under-utilized City-owned Property (AC-1)*): Ruth Spalding, 2109 Medford, discussed the lack of integration of the Independent Community Police Oversight Commission into the City's decision-making processes.
- **Tom Stulberg** (*Temporary Moratorium on all New Development or Expansion of Existing Development that Requires Site Plan Approval in the C1A and C1A/R Zoning Districts, or Rezonings to the C1A or C1A/R Zoning Districts (DC-9)*): Tom Stulberg, 1202 Traver St., spoke in favor of DC-9 and the need for a comprehensive review of the zoning requirements in the City.
- **Ralph McKee** (*Temporary Moratorium on all New Development or Expansion of Existing Development that Requires Site Plan Approval in the C1A and C1A/R Zoning Districts, or Rezonings to the C1A or C1A/R Zoning Districts (DC-9)*): Ralph McKee, 1116 Red Oak, urged City Council to follow the lead of Dr. Lisa Jackson regarding the City's Police Union contract. He also spoke in support of DC-9. He urged Council to postpone DS-1 to gather additional input on the project.

**General Public Comment** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMj4aLWGHrk&t=19358s)):
- Joe Spalding spoke about the need to readdress zoning in the City in order promote affordable housing.
- Ralph McKee, 1116 Red Oak, discussed the lack of public engagement surrounding the redevelopment of 415 W. Washington.
- A caller on behalf of Survivor Speaks discussed the behavior of Councilmembers Griswold and Hayner.
- Kelly Vekermet-Smith discussed the online behavior of Council and the comparison made by some members regarding low incoming housing and chicken coops.
