### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UqSqseg0T5g&t=978s)):
- **[Trische Duckworth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UqSqseg0T5g&t=1056s)** (*Resolution to Approve a 2020 CARES Act Agreement with the Michigan Department of Transportation for Reimbursing Eligible Airport Operating Expenses at the Ann Arbor Municipal Airport ($69,000.00) - (CA-4)*): Trische Duckworth, from Survivors Speak, spoke about race issues in Washtenaw County and closing the gap of inequity between races.
- **[Sandra Andrade](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UqSqseg0T5g&t=1319s)** (*Resolution to Direct the City Administrator and City Attorney to Conduct Due Diligence and Prepare an Ordinance for Dissolution of the DDA - (DC-4)*): Sandra Andrade, representing the Main Street Area Association, spoke in opposition to the resolution to dissolve the DDA. She spoke about the work of the DDA to support downtown businesses during the pandemic, and their overall benefit to the community.
- **Jihye Kim** (*Resolution to Direct the City Administrator and City Attorney to Conduct Due Diligence and Prepare an Ordinance for Dissolution of the DDA - (DC-4)*): Jihye Kim, resident and business owner in Kerrytown, spoke in opposition to the resolution to dissolve the DDA.
- **[Adam Goodman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UqSqseg0T5g&t=1624s)** (*Resolution to Direct the City Administrator and City Attorney to Conduct Due Diligence and Prepare an Ordinance for Dissolution of the DDA - (DC-4)*): Adam Goodman, Ward 5 resident, spoke in opposition of the resolutions regarding the dissolution of the DDA.
- **[Nancy Stone](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UqSqseg0T5g&t=1801s)** (*Resolution to Accept the Revised Solid Waste Resources Management Plan (SWRMP) - (DC-2)*): Nancy Stone, Ward 5 resident and former City employee, spoke in support of DC-1, regarding Solid Waste Resources training.
- **Paige Comagner** (*Resolution to Direct the City Administrator and City Attorney to Conduct Due Diligence and Prepare an Ordinance for Dissolution of the DDA - (DC-4)*): Not present.
- **Peggy Rabhi** (*Resolution Accelerating Development of the Center of the City Community Commons - (DC-5)*): Not present.
- **[Geoffrey Henry](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UqSqseg0T5g&t=2027s)** (*Resolution Accelerating Development of the Center of the City Community Commons - (DC-5)*): Geoffrey Henny, resident, spoke in support of the Resolution Accelerating Development of the Center of the City Community Commons.
- **Shannon Hautamaki** (*Resolution Accelerating Development of the Center of the City Community Commons - (DC-5)*): Shannon Hautamaki, resident, expressed concerns about DC-5, the acceleration of the development of the Center of the City Community Commons.
- **Alan Haber** (*Resolution Accelerating Development of the Center of the City Community Commons - (DC-5)*): Mr. Haber did not speak.

### Public Hearings ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UqSqseg0T5g&t=3804s))
- **[`PH-1`](https://a2gov.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=4624846&GUID=FF2601A5-7F53-4B92-8B85-F622BF8F591C&G=505481FE-B2ED-4B34-9A92-FDB26536D1E1&Options=&Search=): An Ordinance to Amend Chapter 55 (Zoning), Zoning of .74 Acres from TWP (Township District) to R1C (Single-Family Dwelling District), 1780 Scio Church Road (CPC Recommendation: Approval - 8 Yeas and 0 Nays) (ORD-20-26)** ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UqSqseg0T5g&t=3890s)) — There being no one present for comment, the Mayor declared the hearing closed.
- **[`PH-2`](https://a2gov.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=4624869&GUID=3B727BA0-68E4-4D67-B4B7-A63F371C74DF&G=505481FE-B2ED-4B34-9A92-FDB26536D1E1&Options=&Search=): An Ordinance to Amend Sections 5.16.4, 5.16.6, 5.24, 5.29.2, 5.33.2, 5.35.2, 5.36.2 and 5.37 of Chapter 55 (Unified Development Code) Of Title V of The Code Of The City Of Ann Arbor - Sign Regulations (ORD-20-27)** ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UqSqseg0T5g&t=3941s)) — There being no one present for comment, the Mayor declared the hearing closed.

**General Public Comment** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UqSqseg0T5g&t=18327s)):
- Zachary Storey, resident, thanked City Clerk Jackie Beaudry for opening the City Clerk satellite office at the UM Museum of Art.
- Louis Vasquez, Ward 1, spoke about the Community Commons, stating tax payer money should not be used to fund it.
- Alan Haber, resident, spoke about the Community Commons.
- Jamie Magiera, Ward 3, spoke about the DDA and pedestrian and biking paths in the downtown.
- Kitty Kahn, resident, thanked Council for the Community Commons vote.
- A Ward 1 resident spoke about the healthy streets program.
- Eric Sturgis, Ward 1, thanked Council for the vote on DC-5 and thanked Councilmember Griswold for her explanation of her intent.
- An unknown speaker spoke about the Library Lot and stated not all residents want a park there.
