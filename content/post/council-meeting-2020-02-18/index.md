### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bFzTN7ErL7g&t=779s)):
- **[Elizabeth Collins](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bFzTN7ErL7g&t=828s)** (*Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Best Practices and Turf Grass Reduction Strategies Resolution (DC-3)*): Elizabeth Collins, resident, spoke in support of DC-3, regarding pest management practices.
- **[Rita Mitchell](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bFzTN7ErL7g&t=901s)** (*Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Best Practices and Turf Grass Reduction Strategies Resolution (DC-3)*): Rita Mitchell, 621 Fifth Street, spoke in support of DC-3 and thanked the City and the Office of Sustainability and Innovations for their work on climate action.
- **Dan Michniewicz** (*Reliability and Functioning of Detroit Edison Streetlights (DC-7)*): Dan Michniewicz, candidate for Ward Five City Council, spoke about the City's management of street lights and in support of DC-7. He stated the City does a better job of managing street lights than DTE. He also stated City staff do make political decisions.
- **[David Silkworth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bFzTN7ErL7g&t=1284s)** (*Council Communications (DC-2)*): David Silkworth, Ward Five, spoke in support of DC-2, regarding Council Communications.
- **Joan Lowenstein** (*Affordable Housing (CA-12)*): Joan Lowenstein, Board Member of Washtenaw Housing Alliance, spoke in support of CA-12 and community goals regarding affordable housing. Ms. Lowenstein also spoke about Council's priorities and the community's values.
- **Rebecca Arends** (*Chief Michael Cox (F-4)*): Rebecca Arends and James Daniel, residents of Arrowwood Hills Cooperative, spoke in support of Police Chief Michael Cox. Ms. Arends thanked Council Members for attending public meetings in their neighborhood and she spoke about their experience in the police chief selection process. She expressed her frustration with the current situation.
- **Juliet Pressel** (*Police Chief Michael Cox and Communications (F-4 and DC-2)*): Juliet Pressel, 2625 Lillian, spoke in support of Police Chief Michael Cox. She expressed frustration that the public does not know why the Chief was suspended. She stated this is an embarrassment and the communications are a disconnect between staff and Council.
- **Michelle Hughes** (*Barton Drive (CA-6, CA-7)*): Michelle Hughes, resident, spoke in support of CA-6 and CA-7, stating the special assessment is necessary to make improvements.
- **Kathleen Stroud** (*Resolution for City Council Communications (DC-2)*): Kathy Stroud, 2271 Placid Way, spoke in support of DC-2, and better communications.
- **Geoffrey Henny** (*Response to Resolution R-19-569 - Center of the City Interim Use (AC-1)*): Geoffrey Henny, resident, spoke about the recommendations of the Center of the City task force. He urged Council to review the recommendations and to support them.

### Public Hearings ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bFzTN7ErL7g&t=3317s))
- **[`PH-1`](https://a2gov.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=4318279&GUID=1D4B074C-ECD2-4465-9CCF-3275EC96B8AF&G=505481FE-B2ED-4B34-9A92-FDB26536D1E1&Options=&Search=): An Ordinance to Amend Section 1:171 of Chapter 8 (Organization of Boards and Commissions) of the Code of the City of Ann Arbor (Ordinance No. ORD-20-03)** ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bFzTN7ErL7g&t=3363s)) — There being no one present for comment, the Mayor declared the hearing closed.
- **[`PH-2`](https://a2gov.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=4324304&GUID=B915EB9E-17CF-414A-ACE6-1C46865F010F&G=505481FE-B2ED-4B34-9A92-FDB26536D1E1&Options=&Search=): Resolution to Approve the Talpos-Knight Annexation, 1.3 Acres, 2731 Newport (CPC Recommendation: Approval - 8 Yeas and 0 Nays)** ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bFzTN7ErL7g&t=3377s)) — There being no one present for comment, the Mayor declared the hearing closed.

**General Public Comment** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bFzTN7ErL7g&t=9492s)):
- Peter Eckstein, resident, spoke about the recent suicides from the City's parking structures. He urged Council to consider permanent fencing solutions to deter future deaths.
- Sally Hart Petersen, Ward One, spoke about the Resolution to Conclude the Employment Agreement with the City Administrator. Ms. Petersen expressed her frustration on this decision and urged Council to reconsider.
- Julie Roth, resident, spoke in opposition to the Council's vote to terminate the City Administrator, stating it was politically motivated.
- Ed Vielmetti, Ward Four, spoke about the termination of the City Administrator and the sections of the separation agreement. He spoke about the non-disparage section of the contract and suggested it be changed to a mutual admiration agreement. He also talked about the non-disclosure agreement.
- Kai Petainen, resident, spoke about the agreement to terminate the City Administrator's employment agreement. He expressed concern with staff being terminated due to politics.
- Frances Todoro, Vice Chair of ICPOC, shared statements from their recent meeting. The statements were related to the administrative leave of Police Chief Michael Cox and the lack of information and transparency.
- Lisa Jackson, Chair of ICPOC, read a statement from their meeting related to the unexpected administrative leave of Police Chief Michael Cox. She stated ICPOC was created to promote trust and transparency and the Commission cannot do its work with this level of secrecy.
- Zachary Storey, resident, spoke about the City's affordable housing issue. He also addressed the termination of the City Administrator, expressing his concern and frustration with the vote.
- Tim Petersen, Ward One, spoke against the vote to terminate the City Administrator.
- Alan Haber, Ward One, stated he came to the meeting to discuss the Community Commons, but was surprised to learn of the vote to terminate the City Administrator.
