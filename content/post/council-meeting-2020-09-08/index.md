### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U9-leeIO4Fw&t=2644s)):
- **[Joe Andraska](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U9-leeIO4Fw&t=2729s)** (*Police Department*): Joe Andraska, resident, spoke about current police policies. He questioned the need for police to be armed on routine calls.
- **Shannon Hautamaki** (*Parks*): Shannon Hautamaki, resident, spoke about the City's recent Every Park Challenge, mentioning some of the highlights of her family's many park visits.
- **Donna Waters** (*"Survivors Speak"*): Donna Waters spoke about the Police Oversight Commission and the need for a commission during the Black Lives Matter movement.
- **Blaine Coleman** (*We want immediate City clean-up of the Gelman contamination.*): Blaine Coleman, resident, spoke about the Gelman Plume clean-up efforts. He stated a proposed settlement is a "sweetheart deal."
- **Mozhgan Savabiesafahani** (*We want immediate City clean-up of the Gelman contamination.*): Mozhgan Savabiesafahani, resident, spoke about the need to clean-up the Gelman Plume.
