### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IvNEdVWCwrk&t=539s)):
- **Charles Ream** (*Resolution Declaring that the Investigation and Arrest of Individuals Involved with Entheogenic Plants, Including Those Scheduled at State and Federal Levels, be the Lowest Priority for the City of Ann Arbor – DC-2*): Charles Ream, resident, spoke in support of DC-2 and urged Council to amend it, if necessary, to gain support for approval.
- **Jon Dean** (*Resolution Declaring that the Investigation and Arrest of Individuals Involved with Entheogenic Plants, Including Those Scheduled at State and Federal Levels, be the Lowest Priority for the City of Ann Arbor – DC-2*): Jon Dean, resident, spoke in support of DC2.
- **Nicolas Glynos** (*Resolution Declaring that the Investigation and Arrest of Individuals Involved with Entheogenic Plants, Including Those Scheduled at State and Federal Levels, be the Lowest Priority for the City of Ann Arbor – DC-2*): Nicolas Glynos, resident, spoke in support of DC-2.
- **Julia Barron** (*Resolution Declaring that the Investigation and Arrest of Individuals Involved with Entheogenic Plants, Including Those Scheduled at State and Federal Levels, be the Lowest Priority for the City of Ann Arbor – DC-2*): Julia Barron, resident, spoke in support of DC-2.
- **Taryn Schiff** (*Resolution Declaring that the Investigation and Arrest of Individuals Involved with Entheogenic Plants, Including Those Scheduled at State and Federal Levels, be the Lowest Priority for the City of Ann Arbor – DC-2*): Taryn Schiff, resident, spoke in support of DC-2.
- **Jess Williams** (*Resolution Declaring that the Investigation and Arrest of Individuals Involved with Entheogenic Plants, Including Those Scheduled at State and Federal Levels, be the Lowest Priority for the City of Ann Arbor – DC-2*): Jess Williams, resident, spoke in support of DC-7.
- **Diana Quinn** (*Resolution Declaring that the Investigation and Arrest of Individuals Involved with Entheogenic Plants, Including Those Scheduled at State and Federal Levels, be the Lowest Priority for the City of Ann Arbor – DC-2*): Diana Quinn, resident, spoke in support of DC-2.
- **Larry Norris** (*Resolution Declaring that the Investigation and Arrest of Individuals Involved with Entheogenic Plants, Including Those Scheduled at State and Federal Levels, be the Lowest Priority for the City of Ann Arbor – DC-2*): Larry Norris, spoke in support of DC-2.
- **Michelle Hughes** (*Resolution to End the Healthy Streets Initiative Outside of Downtown – DC-7*): Michelle Hughes, resident, spoke in opposition to DC-7 and urged Council to allow citizens to have additional time to adapt to the change in available infrastructure and see the experiment through the allotted time period.
- **Megan Brovan** (*Resolution to End the Healthy Streets Initiative Outside of Downtown – DC-7*): Megan Brovan, resident, spoke in support of DC-7, urging Council to end the program early.

### Public Hearings ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IvNEdVWCwrk&t=4823s))
- **[`PH-1`](https://a2gov.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=4636673&GUID=2EF2AB45-F16C-4A98-A3BE-8575E707EEE4&G=505481FE-B2ED-4B34-9A92-FDB26536D1E1&Options=&Search=): Resolution to Approve the Wade Annexation, .4 Acres, 559 Riverview Drive (CPC Recommendation: Approval - 9 Yeas and 0 Nays)** ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IvNEdVWCwrk&t=4922s)) — There being no one who wished to speak, the Mayor declared the hearing closed.
- **[`PH-2`](https://a2gov.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=4636678&GUID=6AF079DB-9DE6-4F08-93DC-A6EB167F61F9&G=505481FE-B2ED-4B34-9A92-FDB26536D1E1&Options=&Search=): Resolution to Approve 841 Broadway PUD Site Plan and Development Agreement, 841 Broadway (CPC Recommendation: Approval - 8 Yeas and 1 Nays)** ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IvNEdVWCwrk&t=5068s)) — Vince Caruso, on behalf of the Allen's Creek Watershed Group, spoke in opposition to the proposed resolution. **General Public Comment** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IvNEdVWCwrk&t=17651s)):
- Joe Spaulding, a California resident, discussed the City's sanctuary city policy and the need for more individuals to be involved in local politics.
- Zachary Storey, a resident, thanked GAO of the University of Michigan for their strike and their stance against the University's reopening plan.
- Eric Sturgis, a resident, thanked Councilmember Bannister for her work on DC-2, and thanked Councilmember Lumm for her work on DC-4.
- A Ward One resident discussed her disappointment in the fact the healthy streets resolution did not pass tonight.
- Michelle Hughes, resident, discussed her disappointment in the Ann Arbor Police contract being passed on consent agenda without discussion.
- Ben Cunningham, a Ward Four resident, thanked Council for passing DC-2.
