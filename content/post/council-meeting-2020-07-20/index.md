### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I41qLaGskBQ&t=699s)):
- **[Stephanie Mercier](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I41qLaGskBQ&t=758s)** (*Resolution to Order Election and to Determine Ballot Question for Charter Amendment for the 2021 Affordable Housing Millage (DC-6)*): Stephanie Mercier, 2045 Churchill, spoke in support of DC-6, a Charter amendment for affordable housing.
- **Lindsey Calka** (*Resolution to Order Election and to Determine Ballot Question for Charter Amendment for the 2021 Affordable Housing Millage (DC-6)*): Lindsey Calka 307 N. State, spoke in support of DC-6, a charter amendment for affordable housing.
- **[Anthony Scott](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I41qLaGskBQ&t=1138s)** (*Resolution to Order Election and to Determine Ballot Question for Charter Amendment for the 2021 Affordable Housing Millage (DC-6)*): Anthony Scott, 312 Huron Street, spoke in support of DC-6, a charter amendment for affordable housing.
- **Kati Dortch** (*Short Term Rentals Ordinance (C-3)*): Kati Dortch, owner of a Ward 4 short-term rental property, spoke in opposition to C-3, a proposed ordinance to regulate short-term rentals.
- **[Noah Hoffman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I41qLaGskBQ&t=1390s)** (*Short Term Rentals Ordinance (C-3)*): Noah Hoffman, 1765 Northbrook Drive, spoke in opposition to C-3, a proposed ordinance to regulate short-term rentals.
- **Heidi Poscher** (*Short Term Rentals Ordinance (C-3)*): Heidi Poscher, 1204 Brooklyn, spoke in opposition to C-3, a proposed ordinance to regulate short-term rentals.
- **Lola Adefeso** (*Short Term Rentals Ordinance (C-3)*): Lola Adefeso, Ward 2 resident, spoke in opposition to C-3, a proposed ordinance to regulate short-term rentals.
- **Ben Ranta** (*Short Term Rentals Ordinance (C-3)*): Ben Ranta, Ward 5 resident, spoke in opposition to C-3, a proposed ordinance to regulate short-term rentals.
- **Carol Skala** (*Short Term Rentals Ordinance (C-3)*): Carol Skala, short-term rental owner, spoke in opposition to C-3, a proposed ordinance to regulate short-term rentals.
- **Dan Ezekiel** (*Resolution to Approve a Services Agreement and Land and Building Lease with Recycle Ann Arbor (RAA) for Materials Recovery Facility (MRF) Transloading, Re-Equipping and Recyclables Processing (10-Year term, with two Potential 3-Year Extensions)*): Dan Ezekiel, resident, spoke in support of CA-9, a resolution to approve a services agreement and land and building lease with Recycle Ann Arbor for the Materials Recovery Facility (MRF).

### Public Hearings ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I41qLaGskBQ&t=4366s))
- **[`PH-1`](https://a2gov.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=4551857&GUID=D0578431-7B74-4D8E-802E-8C233F5C4901&G=505481FE-B2ED-4B34-9A92-FDB26536D1E1&Options=&Search=): An Ordinance to Amend Chapter 55 (Unified Development Code), Zoning of 4.5 Acres from PUD (Planned Unit Development) to C3 (Fringe Commercial district), MMG Plymouth Road Mixed Use Rezoning, 3611-3621 Plymouth Road, (CPC Recommendation: Approval - 9 Yeas and 0 Nays) (Ordinance No. ORD-20-19)** ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I41qLaGskBQ&t=4750s)) — Wendy Carman, resident, spoke in opposition to the proposed rezoning. There being no further comment, the Mayor declared the hearing closed.
- **[`PH-2`](https://a2gov.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=4587664&GUID=B3B1C121-8CA6-4AAF-A168-E414F29C660F&G=505481FE-B2ED-4B34-9A92-FDB26536D1E1&Options=&Search=): Resolution to Approve MMG Plymouth Road Mixed Use Planned Project Site Plan and Development Agreement, 3611-3621 Plymouth Road (CPC Recommendation: Approval - 9 Yeas and 0 Nays)** ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I41qLaGskBQ&t=4750s)) — Doraid Markus, representing the developer, was present to answer any questions of Council. There being no further comment, the Mayor declared the hearing closed.
- **[`PH-3`](https://a2gov.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=4582117&GUID=5E426F4F-78F5-4621-811E-42D0F7DE794E&G=505481FE-B2ED-4B34-9A92-FDB26536D1E1&Options=&Search=): An Ordinance to Amend Section 1:312(20) of Chapter 14 (Purchasing, Contracting and Selling Procedure) of Title I of the Code of the City of Ann Arbor (Ordinance No. ORD-20-21)** ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I41qLaGskBQ&t=4925s)) — Robert George, resident, spoke in support of the proposed ordinance, on behalf of labor unions. There being no further comment, the Mayor declared the hearing closed.
