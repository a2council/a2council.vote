### Public Comment

**Public Commentary (Reserved Time)** ([start](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PS6Lb-9PkYw&t=457s)):
- **Shannon Hautamaki** (*Resolution to Promote Safe Social Distancing Outdoors in Ann Arbor (DC-5) & Resolution to Move Forward with Lane Closures/Road Closures and Open Streets (DC-7)*): Shannon Hautamaki, 1110 Brooklyn Avenue, thanked Council for continuing to hold Council meetings electronically and urged the City Council to pass DC-5.
- **Mozhgan Savabieasfahani** (*My First 100 Days in Office*): Mozhgan Savabieasfahani, candidate for 4th Ward City Council, discussed what she planned to accomplish in her first 100 days in office should she win election.
- **Blaine Coleman** (*We are against Military Aid to Israel*): Blaine Coleman, PO Cox 7038, urged Council to take action regarding the conflict in Palestine.
- **Henry Herskovitz** (*The Holocaust Article in the April 30th Edition of the Ann Arbor News*): Henry Herskovitz, 250 Highlake Avenue, discussed an article in the April 30th edition of the Ann Arbor News regarding the Holocaust.
- **John Mirsky** (*A2Zero Carbon Neutrality Plan*): John Mirsky, 505 Burson Place, urged Council to revisit their alterations to the A2Zero Net Neutrality plan.
- **Geoff Henny** (*Center of the City (DC-8)*): Geoff Henny, 1402 Henry Street, urged City Council to accept the Center of the City report and start work on the recommendations contained in the report.
- **Michelle Hughes** (*Fair and Equal Michigan (DC-2)*): Michelle Hughes, 325 Charles Street, urged Council to pass the resolution in support of the Fair and Equal Michigan ballot initiative (DC-2).
- **David Shinabarger** (*Resolution to Promote Safe Social Distancing Outdoors in Ann Arbor (DC-5)*): David Shinabarger, a Ward One resident, urged Council to adopt DC-5 in an effort to make streets more bicycle and pedestrian friendly.
